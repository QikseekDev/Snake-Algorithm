Settings

MAX mode maximises learning quality per second: it keeps the target network stable, samples a balanced mixture of apple / death / danger / ordinary transitions, measures real progress with exploration switched off, and only widens exploration when greedy evaluation actually stalls.

In MAX mode the GPU is the gradient engine when WebGPU is available, while CPU cores stay busy on parallel actors. If WebGPU is unavailable, the learner falls back to CPU.

Off by default, since it changes the scoring conditions mid-run. Turn it on to let the move limit self-tune every 10 episodes.

Rewards are normalised to roughly [-1,+1] (apple +1, death -1, small step cost, potential-based food shaping) so Q-values cannot explode. n-step returns, discount, replay mixture quotas and the exploration controller are fixed in code — see cfgRead() and EXPLORE in the script.
